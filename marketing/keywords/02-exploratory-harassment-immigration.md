# Keywords — exploratory: harassment & immigration

**This is not a campaign plan. It is a fact-finding list.**

Paul has asked whether the firm could get traffic on sexual harassment and
immigration. Neither appears anywhere on the site or in any of the four bios.
Before anyone designs a funnel, the useful thing is to see what those clicks
actually cost in Florida and how much search volume exists — so the decision is
made against numbers rather than in the abstract.

Run these through Keyword Planner alongside list 01. If harassment CPCs come back
at $60–80 against thin volume, the decision largely makes itself and nobody has
to argue about it.

> ⚠️ **The blocking question is not cost — it is competence.** Does the firm
> *practice* these areas, hire for them, or refer them out? Referral changes what
> may be advertised at all, and Florida has rules on both holding out a practice
> area and dividing fees. See `../OPEN-QUESTIONS.md`. Nothing here should be built
> until Paul answers that.

## Platform constraints that apply if C proceeds

**Meta will reject accusatory copy.** Its Personal Attributes policy forbids
implying knowledge of a sensitive attribute — *"Were you sexually harassed at
work?"* is a textbook rejection. Copy must stay third-person and general:
*"Workplace harassment claims in Florida — know your rights."* This is the single
most common reason harassment campaigns stall before they start.

**A separate landing surface and phone number.** Mixing plaintiff-side employment
intake into a tax-first premium brand confuses both.

**A different Paula.** Trauma-informed, no qualifier modal, fast handoff to a
human. The existing flow asks for income and net worth — completely wrong here.

**A deadline screener on intake.** EEOC and FCHR windows are the case. Missing one
is not a lost lead, it is a lost claim.
