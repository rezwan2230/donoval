# Membership — how to admit, change and remove a member

**For:** Donovan Legal PLLC office staff · **System:** Clio · **Prepared by:** TICO AI

Membership is controlled entirely from Clio. There is nothing to do on the website,
no password to share, and no list to keep anywhere else.

---

## Admit a member

1. Open the person's **contact** in Clio
2. Set **Membership Tier** to **Gold**, **Platinum**, **Diamond** or **Reserve**
3. Save

That is the whole job. Access is live immediately.

**Then tell them.** Setting the field does not send anything — no email, no
notification. Let the new member know by email, phone or text, and tell them two
things:

> - the web address of their engagement level
> - **the email address to use — the one on their Clio contact.** That address is
>   how the website recognises them. A different one will not work.

## Change someone's level

Change the dropdown to the new level and save. It takes effect the next time they
sign in.

## Remove a member

Clear the **Membership Tier** field and save. Their access ends the next time they
sign in.

**No other member is affected.** Nothing is shared between members, so there is no
password to change and nobody else to notify.

---

## What the member sees

| Situation | What happens on the website |
|---|---|
| They open their own level | It opens |
| They open a level they don't hold | *"You are a Platinum member"* — with a button into the level they do hold |
| They are not a member | The public page for that level, and an invitation to request access |
| Their level was cleared | They are treated as not a member |

Nobody ever reaches a dead end. Someone who is not a member is offered a
consultation, which is the firm's normal intake.

---

## If a member says it isn't working

Check these three, in order. One of them is almost always the answer.

**1. Is Membership Tier actually set on their contact?**
A blank field means no access.

**2. Are they using the email address on that Clio contact?**
This is the most common cause. The website matches on that address exactly. A
personal address when the contact holds a work one — or the other way round — will
not be recognised.

**3. Does that email address appear on more than one contact?**
If it does, the website refuses access **on purpose**, because it cannot tell which
person is signing in. Merge or correct the duplicate contact and it will work.

If none of those explains it, contact TICO.

---

## Two things worth knowing

**Membership is a single field.** There is no separate "active" or "paid" tick box.
Blank means not a member; a level means an active member. One field, one state,
nothing that can get out of step.

**Access can be ended.** Clearing the field ends that member's access without
touching anyone else. That is the difference between selling access to something
and giving away a copy of it.

---

## What this does not do

- **It does not send anything to the member.** Admitting someone is silent — always
  tell them yourself.
- **It does not bill or invoice.** Membership terms are handled by the firm as
  usual.
- **It does not create the person.** The contact has to exist in Clio first.
