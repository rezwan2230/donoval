## `[REAL ESTATE]` Real estate topic primers (general education — NOT advice)
- **Real Estate Professional Status (REPS, §469(c)(7))** — two gates: >750 hours in real property trades, AND >50% of all personal services in those trades; met by one spouse alone; contemporaneous time logs are essential. Determines whether rental losses are usable against other income.
- **FIRPTA (§1445)** — foreign sellers of U.S. real estate face 15% withholding on the gross sale price; a Form 8288-B withholding certificate can reduce it toward the actual tax. The buyer is the withholding agent and is personally liable.
- **§1031 like-kind exchanges** — defer gain into replacement property; 45-day identification and 180-day exchange windows; forward and reverse structures.
- **Cost segregation & bonus depreciation** — reclassifies building components into shorter recovery periods, accelerating depreciation; placed-in-service timing is a planning lever.
- **The Augusta Rule (§280A(g))** — rent your residence to your business for **fewer than 15 days/year**: rental income excluded, business deducts fair market rent. Day 15 is a cliff; needs market-rate support and documented meetings.
- **Short-term rentals & Opportunity Zones** — STR material-participation strategy; OZ deferral with attention to the program's timeline.

## `[TAX]` Tax controversy topic primers (general education — NOT advice)
- **The Tax Controversy Roadmap** — a federal tax dispute moves through stations: processing → **assessment** (starts the 10-year collection clock, the CSED) → exam → Appeals → collection → litigation. Statutory deadlines govern each stage; missing a response window can permanently close a forum.
- **The notice that matters most** — the **Final Notice of Intent to Levy** (Letter 1058 / LT11 / CP90) opens a **30-day Collection Due Process (CDP)** window (Form 12153) that suspends the levy and routes to Appeals. A **CP504 is NOT the final notice** and carries no CDP rights — a common, costly confusion.
- **Liens vs. levies** — a lien claims priority; a levy seizes property (wages, bank accounts).
- **Collection alternatives** — installment agreement, partial-pay installment, currently-not-collectible (hardship), and offer in compromise — keyed to ability to pay.
- **Trust Fund Recovery Penalty (§6672)** — unpaid withheld payroll "trust-fund" taxes can become a responsible person's **personal** liability; Letter 1153 opens a 60-day protest window.
- **IRS audit notice — first steps** — distinguish a 30-day letter from a 90-day Statutory Notice of Deficiency (jurisdictional, non-extendable to Tax Court); don't ignore notices; the original CPA is a fact witness, and §7525 privilege is narrower than attorney-client and doesn't apply in criminal matters.
