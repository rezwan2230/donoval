Screenshots were generated in Chromium with two viewports:

- **Desktop:** 1440 × 1100 viewport, full-page screenshot.
- **Mobile:** 390 × 844 viewport, full-page screenshot.

No forms were submitted. No private or password-protected areas were accessed.
