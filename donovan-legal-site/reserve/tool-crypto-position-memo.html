Crypto Position Memo

An educational digital-asset memo generator. Each position is characterized as property under IRS Notice 2014-21, scored for long- versus short-term holding period, and measured for unrealized gain or loss. The tool then flags potential Form 8938 (§ 6038D) and FBAR (FinCEN Form 114) reporting where custody is foreign and value crosses the documented filing thresholds. Every threshold is labeled with its source and year. This is an educational summary, not legal or tax advice.

The authorities this tool encodes — Notice 2014-21, § 1222, § 1091, § 6038D, 31 CFR 1010.350

Character — property, not currency

For U.S. federal tax purposes, virtual currency is treated as property. General tax principles applicable to property transactions apply to transactions using digital assets.

Source: IRS Notice 2014-21, 2014-16 I.R.B. 938, A-1 (2014); reaffirmed by Rev. Rul. 2019-24 and the annual Form 1040 digital-asset question.

Holding period — long- vs short-term

A capital asset held for more than one year yields long-term treatment on disposition; held one year or less, short-term. The holding period begins the day after acquisition.

Source: IRC § 1222(1)–(4); holding-period rule, Rev. Rul. 66-7 (2026 tool build).

Wash-sale rule — § 1091

The wash-sale loss-deferral rule applies to “stock or securities.” Because digital assets are treated as property rather than securities, § 1091 does not currently apply to crypto. Legislative proposals have sought to extend it; none is enacted as of this build.

Source: IRC § 1091(a); IRS Notice 2014-21 (property characterization). Current law as of 2026 — confirm before relying.

Foreign-asset reporting — Form 8938 & FBAR

Specified foreign financial assets are reported on Form 8938 above status-based thresholds; foreign financial accounts aggregating over $10,000 are reported on the FBAR. Directly-held (self-custody) crypto is generally not a reportable financial account; crypto held through a foreign institution may be.

Source: IRC § 6038D & IRS Instructions for Form 8938 (Rev. Nov. 2021); 31 CFR § 1010.350 & FinCEN Form 114; FinCEN Notice 2020-2.
