# Donovan Legal PLLC — crawl and AI policy
#
# Nothing here is BLOCKED that was not blocked before. The wildcard below already
# allowed every crawler; naming these agents only makes that permission explicit.
#
# Why bother: Google-Extended and Applebot-Extended are opt-OUT controls for AI
# training and answer surfaces, so a site that never names them is relying on a
# default the vendor can change. Several AI crawlers also treat an explicit Allow
# as a stronger signal than a wildcard. For a firm whose content strategy is
# publishing tax commentary that people ask AI tools about, being citable is the
# whole point.
#
# Google-Extended affects AI use ONLY. It is not Googlebot and has no effect on
# ordinary Search ranking in either direction.
#
# Members-only material is gated behind authentication, not behind this file, so
# nothing here exposes anything that was protected.
