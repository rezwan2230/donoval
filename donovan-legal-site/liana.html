LIANA CAJADO

Strategic Advisor · Cape Cod & Martha’s Vineyard

Liana Cajado is Strategic Advisor — Cape Cod & Martha’s Vineyard at Donovan Legal PLLC, and the founder of MV Tax Services, a tax and financial services firm she has built since 2011 into a practice serving hundreds of clients across Martha’s Vineyard, Cape Cod, and throughout the United States. She works from offices in Falmouth and Edgartown, Massachusetts.

Her practice spans tax preparation, bookkeeping, payroll, and insurance, with particular depth in the Cape and Islands’ year-round and seasonal communities — a market where the tax picture routinely crosses state lines, mixes wage and self-employment income, and involves seasonal property in ways that reward local knowledge. MV Tax Services also provides Brazilian consular services, reflecting her longstanding role in the Vineyard’s substantial Brazilian community.

Ms. Cajado serves on the board of the Communication Ambassador Partnership, a Martha’s Vineyard organization dedicated to bridging language access across the Island and beyond.

For Donovan Legal PLLC, she is the firm’s presence in the Cape Cod and Martha’s Vineyard market. The firm’s Massachusetts practice — Mr. Donovan is admitted to the Massachusetts Bar, holds a Massachusetts CPA license, and developed residential real estate in the Commonwealth — pairs with her established local presence: clients on the Cape and Islands whose matters call for tax counsel reach the firm through someone they already know, and the firm’s clients with Cape and Islands interests gain a trusted local resource.

Ms. Cajado is not an attorney. Services she provides on behalf of Donovan Legal PLLC are performed under the direct supervision of Mr. Donovan, and all legal advice is rendered solely by an attorney admitted to practice. MV Tax Services is an independent firm, and its services are provided separately from, and are not supervised by, Donovan Legal PLLC.
