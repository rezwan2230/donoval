Important — please read before contacting the firm:

Sending a message to Donovan Legal PLLC does not establish an attorney-client relationship. Until the firm has performed a conflicts check and the firm and the prospective client have signed a written engagement agreement, no attorney-client relationship exists, and the firm has no duty of confidentiality with respect to information the prospective client provides.

Please do not include confidential or sensitive information in your initial message. Limit your inquiry to a brief description of the matter and your contact information. The firm will respond to schedule a conversation, run a conflicts check, and, if appropriate, send an engagement letter before any confidential information is exchanged.
