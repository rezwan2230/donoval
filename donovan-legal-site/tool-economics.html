RESERVE-Tier Tool

Deal Economics Tool

Financial-feasibility analysis for real-estate deals across thirty deal-type archetypes: for-sale residential, hold residential, commercial, hospitality, mixed-use, ground-up development, and specialty (NNN, LIHTC, QOZ, sale-leaseback, land entitlement). Generates a feasibility memorandum, sources & uses, capital stack, multi-year pro forma with DSCR and debt yield, deal-level IRR and MOIC, sensitivity grid, break-even analysis, and a 10-tab Excel workbook with live formulas.

Access is restricted to Donovan Legal RESERVE members. Member firms reach the tool at donovan.law/reserve/ with their basic-auth credentials.
