CLIENT PORTAL

Donovan Legal PLLC clients reach their matters through Clio for Clients, the secure portal of the firm’s practice-management system: documents we have shared with you, documents you send us, messages, calendar events, and invoices you can view and pay.

Sign in at clients.clio.com

There is no password. Enter the email address the firm has on file for you and Clio sends a one-time sign-in link from donotreply@clio.com; the link works once and expires after an hour. Request a new one whenever you return.

Clio for ClientsSign in to the portal iPhone and AndroidGet the app

Three things to know

  • Your portal opens when we open it. Access begins the first time the firm shares a document or message with you. If you are a client and have not received an invitation, call the office at (561) 666-6022 or email info@donovan.law and we will send one.
  • Use the email address on file. The sign-in link goes only to the address associated with your matter. If you have changed addresses, tell us before you try to sign in.
  • Everything in the portal is confidential. Clio for Clients is the secure channel for sending us tax returns, financial statements and other documents; please do not send those by ordinary email.
