REAL ESTATE DEVELOPMENT

“Our 200-unit luxury apartment development in Boca Raton faced significant construction delays, budget overruns, and mounting pressure from both our investors and lender. With the project behind schedule and the Certificate of Occupancy still outstanding, the successful sale of the property was at serious risk.

Paul Donovan stepped in during a critical stage of the project and immediately brought order and direction to an extremely challenging situation. He coordinated the completion of the remaining subcontractor work, managed the municipal inspection process, negotiated contractor claims and lien releases, and successfully guided the project through to obtaining the Certificate of Occupancy.

Paul’s ability to navigate complex construction, legal, and operational issues was instrumental in allowing us to complete the transaction and close the sale of the property. His professionalism, responsiveness, and strategic approach gave our investors and lending partners confidence during a very difficult period. I would highly recommend Paul to any developer or property owner involved in sophisticated real estate projects.”

— Mike Mele
President, Mele Development
