What the Deal Builder Does

Capital Stack

Unlimited member classes with independent capital, preferred return rate, profit share, loss share, voting rights, and transfer restrictions.

  • Class A, B, C, D… with custom labels and color-coded badges
  • Guarantor LP class with enhanced pref and guaranty fee support
  • Sponsor / GP class with promote-only economics
  • Senior debt + member debt + mezzanine tracked separately
  • Real-time LTC, equity %, and capital stack totals

Waterfall Engine

Configure the distribution mechanics with the precision a modern deal requires.

  • American (deal-by-deal) and European (fund-level) styles
  • Multi-class preferred return (cumulative simple, compound, non-cumulative)
  • Multiple promote tiers with IRR or MOIC hurdles
  • GP catch-up: 50/50, 80/20, or full to sponsor target
  • Clawback (full, partial, with interest, minimum return threshold)
  • Per-tier trace showing dollars and remaining pool

Debt under § 752

Member debt classification and allocation, integrated with the deal economics.

  • Nonrecourse, recourse, qualified nonrecourse financing under § 465(b)(6), and partner nonrecourse debt under Reg. § 1.704-2(b)(4)
  • Senior construction loan + mezzanine + member loan stacking
  • Interest carry computed off average outstanding balance
  • Treatment notes per classification surfaced inline

Governance & Provisions

Capture the structural framework that becomes the operating agreement.

  • Manager-managed, board-managed, member-managed, or sponsor-as-GP
  • Major decision matrix with required vote threshold
  • ROFR, ROFO, tag-along, drag-along, buy-sell, put/call, key-man
  • Promote clawback with personal guaranty

State Tax Engine

50 states plus DC with marginal income tax rate, capital gains rate, and conformity flags for § 168(k) bonus, § 163(j) interest limitation, § 461(l) excess business loss, and § 199A QBI. PTET election availability flagged where applicable.

Results, Sensitivity, Export

Verdict card with PENCILS / MARGINAL / DOES NOT PENCIL output. Sources & uses table. Returns by class with MOIC and IRR. Waterfall trace tier-by-tier. 5×5 sensitivity heat map flexing exit price and hold period. Structural diagram. Notes engine surfacing securities, § 752, dealer-property, and state-conformity issues automatically. Save / load JSON, print summary, export term sheet for circulation.
