A NOTICE IS A CLOCK

Most people who receive a tax notice put it aside. That is the one thing the system does not forgive.

Almost every door in a tax dispute is gated by a statutory deadline running from the date printed on a notice, and some of those doors close in as few as thirty days. A response window that closes does not reopen. The forum behind it is simply gone, and the matter proceeds without the argument that would have been made there.

The first question in any controversy engagement is therefore the same: what is the date on your notice?
